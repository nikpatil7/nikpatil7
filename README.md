# 💫 About Me
I'm a final-year IT student @ DY Patil COE. I work primarily with the **MERN stack, Java, C++, and SQL**, often turning bugs into features 😅. I love building web apps that actually work (most of the time).  

From **MindForge Codex** (an e-learning platform) to **ClaimHub** (a lost & found system), I enjoy transforming ideas into scalable solutions. Proud **Cummins Scholar**, full-time problem solver, part-time debugger, and always learning 🚀.  

💡 Just another dev who still thinks `console.log()` is a debugger.  

I enjoy leading projects, solving challenging problems, and experimenting with new technologies. Always open to collaboration, mentorship, and new challenges!
